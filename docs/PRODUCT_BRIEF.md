# MandiMate — Product Brief (source vision)

> Better deals. Shared logistics. More money for farmers.

MandiMate is a farmer-first marketplace that combines small harvests from multiple farmers into
buyer-ready lots, negotiates better selling opportunities, coordinates transport, and helps farmers
understand exactly how much money they will earn. It is a smart deal-making assistant for agriculture,
not just another app showing crop prices. A farmer shouldn't have to figure out buyers, negotiate alone,
arrange transport, calculate deductions and chase payments independently — MandiMate brings those
activities together in one place.

## 1. The problem
A village where 20 farmers grow tomatoes: Farmer A has 200 kg ready, Farmer B 350 kg, Farmer C 500 kg.
A nearby retailer or wholesale buyer wants 1,000 kg of consistent-quality tomatoes. Transporting each
farmer's produce separately costs more than collecting it together. Each farmer has produce to sell, but
may not individually have the quantity, buyer connections, bargaining power or logistics to secure a good
deal. MandiMate identifies compatible harvests, combines them into a consignment, finds suitable buyers,
compares offers and coordinates the transaction.

A farmer's actual earnings depend on the price received, transportation, commissions, spoilage, rejected
produce, payment reliability and other costs. **Mission: maximise the farmer's net earnings from every
harvest, not merely the quoted selling price.**

## 2. Users
1. **Farmers** — list harvests, understand selling options, join pooled sales, see expected earnings and
   track transactions. Local languages and assisted use by a field worker.
2. **Buyers** — retailers, wholesalers, processors, hotels, institutional kitchens: describe needs, submit
   offers, source from a coordinated group of farmers.
3. **Transporters** — see pickup requests, estimate transport requirements, consolidate deliveries and quote.
4. **FPOs and field coordinators** — register farmers, verify harvest information, organise collections and
   help resolve disputes.

## 3. The journey (8 steps)
1. Farmer registers a harvest — crop, quantity, quality, location, harvest date, sell-by.
2. MandiMate checks and groups harvests — compatible lots by crop, grade, location, quantity, delivery time.
3. Buyers submit offers — price, quantity, quality standards, pickup terms, payment conditions.
4. MandiMate compares real earnings — estimated net proceeds per farmer after transport, handling, deductions, fees.
5. Farmers review the proposed deal — best feasible options; accept or reject. No sale without consent.
6. Transport and pickup are coordinated — collection points, vehicle capacity, pickup sequence, timing.
7. Produce is delivered and checked — delivered quantity, quality, shortages, rejections, discrepancies.
8. Settlement and results are tracked — sale amounts, deductions, payment status, earnings vs the alternative.

The innovation is connecting these eight steps into one workflow: a listing platform stops at discovery;
MandiMate continues until produce is delivered and the actual financial outcome is recorded.

## 4. Features
- **Farmer**: harvest listing; voice-based listing (Telugu and other languages); photo-assisted listing;
  private minimum acceptable (net) price; lot pooling; buyer comparison by net earnings; sale tracking;
  earnings history; alerts (harvest deadlines, expiring offers, pickup times).
- **Buyer**: post requirements; browse aggregated supply; submit and negotiate offers with quality specs and
  payment terms; track orders; record delivery and quality checks; pay settlements.
- **Aggregation & deals**: compatible-lot matching; pooled lots; net-return calculator; offer comparison;
  counter-offers; farmer consent; deal confirmation.
- **Transport & delivery**: pickup requests; route & pickup sequence; vehicle capacity planning; transporter
  quotes; pickup & delivery confirmation.
- **Trust, payments & operations**: buyer/transporter verification; settlement & payment tracking; payout
  records; disputes; audit trail; assisted onboarding by coordinators.

## 5. Where AI is used
1. **Voice-to-harvest assistant** (high priority) — "I have 400 kilos of tomatoes ready for pickup tomorrow
   morning" → draft listing; asks for missing details instead of guessing.
2. **Produce photo assistant** (optional) — preliminary description of visible characteristics. A photo
   cannot establish grade, weight, freshness or food safety; final acceptance follows inspection.
3. **Smart lot matching** (core) — explains why a group of lots is a promising match and identifies missing
   information. Final matching and quantity checks follow explicit rules; AI never silently combines
   incompatible produce or invents supply.
4. **Deal negotiation assistant** (core differentiator) — explains trade-offs ("Offer A pays ₹2 more per kg,
   but its transport and handling costs are ₹1.50 more per kg; Offer B pays sooner") and suggests a
   counter-offer based on the farmer's minimum and verified market information. Prices, costs and terms
   come from reliable data, never AI guesses.
5. **Supply & demand assistant** (later) — recurring requirements, seasonal patterns, surplus periods.

MandiMate combines **AI for understanding** (voice, languages, photos, messy information), **rules and
calculations for decisions** (compatibility, transport costs, net returns, feasibility) and **people for
trust** (verification, inspection, consent, disputes, payment resolution).

## 6. What the farmer sees
A farmer must understand a deal in seconds: their current harvest ("400 kg Tomatoes · Ready tomorrow ·
Quality pending inspection"), their selling options (e.g. Offer A · Local trader ₹14/kg vs Offer B · Pooled
sale ₹16/kg) and an AI deal explanation — always **net** earnings, clearly distinguishing confirmed terms,
estimates and unresolved conditions.

## 7. Differentiation
| Typical marketplace | MandiMate |
|---|---|
| Shows buyers and sellers | Coordinates a complete transaction |
| Lists prices | Calculates estimated net proceeds |
| Treats each listing separately | Finds compatible lots across farmers |
| Leaves transport to the user | Coordinates consolidated pickup |
| Stops at buyer discovery | Tracks delivery, deductions and settlement |
| Generic recommendations | Explains why a particular deal is better or worse |

Defensible advantage over time: verified buyer network, reliable transaction histories, local logistics
knowledge, and evidence about what actually improves farmers' earnings.

## 8. Business model
No barrier for farmers. Buyer-side coordination fee per successfully sourced lot; FPO/cooperative
subscriptions (aggregation, buyer management, records, settlement tracking); transparent logistics
coordination fee. Illustration (hypothetical, not a forecast): 100 consignments/month × ₹500 = ₹50,000
monthly gross revenue before operating costs.

## 9. Launch
One location (village cluster), one crop, one buyer segment, one measurable problem, one complete
workflow. Pilot: 10–20 farmers, a few verified buyers, one or two transport partners (targets, not claims).
Measure: additional net income per kg vs a credible alternative; % of listed produce sold; time from listing
to confirmed buyer; transport cost per kg; payment delays, rejections, failed transactions; repeat usage.

## 10. Phases
1. Hackathon MVP — listings, offers, lot grouping, net-return comparison, consent, simulated deal confirmation, one AI feature.
2. Real pilot — verified buyers, real transport quotes, field agents, quality checks, delivery confirmation, payment tracking.
3. Growing business — more crops, repeat-order matching, demand insights, logistics planning, more languages, FPO partnerships.
4. Scale — more districts and buyer categories, deeper analytics, agri-commerce & logistics network integrations.

## 11. Risks & mitigations
- Not enough buyers → validate demand before recruiting many farmers.
- Poor quality consistency → pool only produce that meets agreed specifications.
- Transport eats the margin → calculate logistics before recommending a deal.
- Farmers distrust the platform → transparency, right to decline, every fee and deduction shown.
- Buyers don't pay → verify counterparties, record payment terms, clear dispute process.
- Too operationally expensive → start with assisted coordination, measure cost per completed sale, automate later.

## 12. Positioning
Don't pitch MandiMate as "an AI platform for farmers". Pitch it as a system that turns fragmented harvests
into viable commercial deals and proves whether farmers actually earn more.
